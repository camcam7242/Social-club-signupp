## 1. INTRODUCTION

MechMarket ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use the MechMarket mobile application ("App").

**Please read this policy carefully. By using the App, you consent to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.**

If you do not agree with this policy, do not use the App.

---

## 2. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly
- **Account Information:** Name, email address, phone number, password
- **Profile Information:** Business name, bio, service radius (Mechanics)
- **Vehicle Information:** Make, model, year, VIN (Customers)
- **Payment Information:** Credit/debit card details (processed and stored by Stripe — we do not store full card numbers)
- **Identity Verification:** Government-issued ID, professional certifications, ASE credentials (Mechanics)
- **Communications:** Messages sent through our in-app chat feature
- **Reviews and Ratings:** Feedback you submit about other users

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically
- **Location Data:** Real-time GPS location when you use the App (required for matching Customers with nearby Mechanics and for live job tracking). We collect location in the foreground and, with your permission, in the background during active jobs.
- **Device Information:** Device type, operating system, unique device identifiers, IP address
- **Usage Data:** Pages viewed, features used, time spent in the App, clicks, and navigation patterns
- **Push Notification Tokens:** To send you job updates, payment confirmations, and account alerts
- **Log Data:** Server logs including timestamps, error reports, and crash data

### 2.3 Information from Third Parties
- **Stripe:** Payment processing status, payout information, identity verification results
- **Google Maps:** Location and routing data used to display maps and calculate distances
- **Expo / Push Notification Services:** Delivery status of push notifications

## 3. HOW WE USE YOUR INFORMATION

We use the information we collect to:

- **Provide the Service:** Match Customers with Mechanics, process bookings and payments, facilitate job completion
- **Process Payments:** Charge Customers and pay out Mechanics via Stripe Connect
- **Location Services:** Show Mechanics your location to travel to you; show Customers the Mechanic's live location during a job
- **Account Management:** Create and maintain your account, verify your identity
- **Safety and Security:** Detect fraud, prevent abuse, enforce our five-strike policy, suspend accounts that violate our Terms
- **Communications:** Send push notifications and in-app messages about bookings, job status, payments, and account alerts (including strike warnings)
- **Customer Support:** Respond to your requests, disputes, and questions
- **Legal Compliance:** Meet our obligations under applicable laws including IRS 1099-K reporting for Mechanics earning $600+
- **App Improvement:** Analyze usage patterns to fix bugs and improve features
- **Marketing:** With your consent, send promotional communications (you may opt out at any time)

## 4. HOW WE SHARE YOUR INFORMATION

We do **not** sell your personal information. We share your information only in the following circumstances:

### 4.1 With Other Users
- When a job is matched, Customers can see the Mechanic's name, business name, rating, tier, and live location during the job
- Mechanics can see the Customer's first name, vehicle info, service location, and job description
- Reviews and ratings are visible to other users

### 4.2 With Service Providers
We share information with third-party vendors who assist us in operating the App:
- **Stripe, Inc.** — Payment processing and identity verification
- **Google LLC** — Maps and location services
- **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** — Cloud hosting and file storage
- **Expo** — Push notification delivery
- **Supabase** — Database hosting

All service providers are contractually required to protect your information and use it only for the purposes we specify.

### 4.3 For Legal Reasons
We may disclose your information if required to:
- Comply with a subpoena, court order, or legal process
- Protect the rights, property, or safety of MechMarket, our users, or the public
- Detect, prevent, or address fraud or security issues
- Enforce our Terms of Service

### 4.4 Business Transfers
If MechMarket is acquired, merged, or sells assets, your information may be transferred as part of that transaction. We will notify you before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy.

### 4.5 With Your Consent
We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

## 5. LOCATION DATA

Location data is central to how MechMarket works. Specifically:

- **Customers:** We collect your location to find nearby Mechanics and to share with your assigned Mechanic so they can navigate to you
- **Mechanics:** We collect your real-time location to show Customers where you are during an active job and to show your availability radius

**You can disable location permissions in your device settings, but doing so will prevent the App from functioning properly.**

We do not sell location data to advertisers. Location data is used solely to operate the platform.

## 6. PUSH NOTIFICATIONS

We send push notifications for:
- New job requests (Mechanics)
- Job status updates (both)
- Payment confirmations (both)
- Strike warnings and account status (Mechanics)
- Chat messages (both)

You can disable push notifications in your device settings at any time. Disabling notifications may cause you to miss time-sensitive job or payment information.

## 7. DATA RETENTION

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App. Specifically:

- **Account data:** Retained until you delete your account, plus 3 years after for legal compliance
- **Transaction records:** Retained for 7 years for tax and legal purposes
- **Chat messages:** Retained for 1 year after the associated job is completed
- **Location history:** Retained for 90 days, then deleted
- **Deleted accounts:** Most data deleted within 30 days of account deletion; transaction records retained per above

## 8. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data transmission (HTTPS/TLS)
- Encrypted storage of sensitive data
- Secure password hashing (bcrypt)
- JWT-based authentication with token expiration
- Stripe-managed PCI-DSS compliant payment processing

**No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.** We cannot guarantee absolute security. If a data breach occurs that affects your rights, we will notify you as required by law.

## 11. CALIFORNIA RESIDENTS — CCPA RIGHTS

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

- **Right to Know:** Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, use, disclose, and sell
- **Right to Delete:** Request deletion of your personal information, subject to certain exceptions
- **Right to Opt-Out of Sale:** We do not sell personal information. No opt-out is needed.
- **Right to Non-Discrimination:** We will not discriminate against you for exercising your CCPA rights

To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us at support@mechmarket.app with the subject line "CCPA Request."

**California Shine the Light:** California Civil Code Section 1798.83 allows California residents to request information about sharing personal information with third parties for direct marketing. We do not share personal information for third-party direct marketing.

## 13. EUROPEAN USERS — GDPR

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you have additional rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

- **Lawful Basis:** We process your data based on contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligation, and/or your consent
- **Right of Access:** Request a copy of your personal data
- **Right to Rectification:** Request correction of inaccurate data
- **Right to Erasure:** Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to Restriction:** Request we limit processing of your data
- **Right to Portability:** Receive your data in a machine-readable format
- **Right to Object:** Object to processing based on legitimate interests
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw at any time

To exercise these rights, contact support@mechmarket.app. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
